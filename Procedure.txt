MedTrack: Setup and Usage Procedure
===================================================

This document outlines the step-by-step procedure required to launch the MedTrack platform locally on your machine and how to operate the mobile application for demonstration purposes.

---------------------------------------------------
PART 1: STARTING THE BACKEND SERVER
---------------------------------------------------
The backend houses the PostgreSQL database connection, AI parsing algorithms (Groq LLaMA-3), and the Sarvam AI Multilingual translation pipeline.

1. Open your terminal or command prompt.
2. Navigate into the backend directory:
   cd backend
3. Ensure all dependencies are installed:
   npm install
4. Ensure your PostgreSQL database is running and your `.env` file is complete with your `DATABASE_URL`, `JWT_SECRET`, `GROQ_API_KEY`, and `SARVAM_API_KEY`.
5. Start the backend Node server:
   npm run dev
6. You should see a terminal message stating "Server initialized..." and "Connected to PostgreSQL". Keep this terminal open! Simulated OTP SMS messages will print here during authentication.

---------------------------------------------------
PART 2: STARTING THE FRONTEND (MOBILE APP)
---------------------------------------------------
The frontend is built on React Native and runs via the Expo CLI.

1. Open a **new**, separate terminal window.
2. Navigate into the frontend directory:
   cd frontend
3. Ensure all dependencies are installed:
   npm install
4. Start the Expo development server:
   npm start
5. A large QR code will appear in your terminal.
6. Install the "Expo Go" app on your physical iPhone or Android device.
   - For Android: Open Expo Go and choose "Scan QR Code".
   - For iPhone: Open the default Camera app, hover over the QR code, and tap the yellow "Open in Expo Go" banner.
7. The MedTrack app will bundle and open on your physical device.

---------------------------------------------------
PART 3: USING THE MEDTRACK APPLICATION
---------------------------------------------------

FLOW A: Doctor Registration & Prescribing
1. Open the App and tap 'Register' down at the bottom.
2. Select the "Doctor" toggle. Enter a mock Name, Email, Phone Number, and Password.
3. Tap "Create Account".
4. Check your Backend Terminal (from Part 1). You will see a mocked SMS text containing a 4-digit OTP.
5. In the App, enter that 4-digit OTP and tap "Verify & Login".
6. In the Doctor Dashboard, create a new profile for a Patient. Add a medication name, dosage amount, timeline (e.g., Daily), duration (in days), and essential food instructions (e.g., After Food, Empty Stomach).
7. Submit the prescription. It is now cryptographically secured on the backend.

FLOW B: Patient Experience & Alarms
1. Log out of the Doctor profile.
2. Log in using the exact phone number you just assigned to the patient (look at the terminal for their new OTP).
3. Welcome to the Patient Dashboard! You will see the active medications the Doctor just assigned to you.
4. **Alarms:** Because MedTrack utilizes background chronotherapeutic tracking, your phone will actively loop a loud Text-To-Speech (voice) notification and a ringtone when it is time to take a pill. This alert will violently persist every few seconds until you physically press "Dismiss" in the app interface.
5. **Custom Ringtones:** If you dislike the default notification sound, you can click to select your own custom MP3 device ringtone which the app will bind natively!

FLOW C: Multilingual AI Diagnostics (Sarvam AI)
1. At any time in the app, look at the top right corner for the "Change Lang:" dropdown.
2. You can actively shift the entire application's native language into English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, or Kannada!
3. In the Patient Dashboard, scroll down and find "Upload Lab Report".
4. Upload a standard medical PDF. 
5. Our system will extract the raw text, scrub it of confidential Personal Identifiable Information (HIPAA-compliant sanitization), and ping LLaMa-3 via Groq to extract the complicated data.
6. Sarvam AI will then seamlessly translate this highly complex diagnostic summary into a perfectly readable, conversational overview in the regional dialect you selected in step 2!

---------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------------------------------------------
- Blank screen on Mobile? Make sure your phone and your computer are connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network!
- Database errors? We created a script in the backend called `clear_db.js`. If you wish to wipe the database clean for a demonstration, simply run `node clear_db.js` in the backend terminal!
